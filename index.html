About & Help

VNHS NaviGo helps visitors, new students, transferees and parents find classrooms, offices and other school facilities.

How to use

  1. Search. Type a room number (116), a section (Shin Saimdang), an adviser (Ducao-Visto) or a place (Clinic) in any search bar. You can also type a grade, like Grade 10.
  2. See the details. Pick a result to see the room number, the sections in it, their advisers, and the building and floor.
  3. Find it on the map. Press View on map. The room is marked with a pin. Drag to move the map, and scroll or pinch to zoom.
  4. Get directions. Press Directions, then choose where you are: a gate, a landmark, or a spot you tap on the map. The walking route is drawn from A (start) to B (destination).
  5. Follow the steps. The Directions panel lists each turn, with distances and nearby landmarks to look out for.

Reading the map

  • Some buildings have more than one floor. On the map, each floor is drawn as its own row or column, marked with a colored badge: 1F 2F 3F 4F.
  • For a room on an upper floor, the route ends at the building entrance. From there, take the stairs up to the floor shown.
  • Walking distances and times are estimates.
